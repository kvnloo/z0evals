## Background

Paper: "Sparks of Weight-Space Programming" (https://rikiyat.github.io/blog/weight-space-programming/), with full logs at
https://github.com/RikiyaT/lm_understand-logs (read exp8_math/README.md, exp8_math/runs/*/final_answer.md and
exp8_math/gemma_followup/). In its Experiment 8 a frontier model edited Qwen3.5-0.8B's weights directly, with no training,
and raised MATH-500 from 7.9 to 48.1 (GSM8K 13.8 -> 67.1) with thinking on. Answers cut at the 32k-token limit fell from
93% to 33%. The winning edits removed a "repetition / loop" direction from attention and MLP output matrices
(W' = W - a * u u^T W) or zeroed one attention head. On Gemma 3 270M, which rarely loops, no edit moved MATH-500 by more
than about a point. So weight editing worked as REPAIR of a named failure mode, not as added capability.

My project needs exactly that capability as a reusable tool: tiny models (135M-0.8B) that I want to use as cheap
decision models have failure modes (looping, never emitting a final answer, majority-class collapse), and I want a
principled, gradient-free way to find and remove them.

## Rules

- No gradient descent, autograd, optimizers or iterative fitting of weights to a loss. Weight analysis, forward passes
  (as many as you like), and directly computed edits (projections, ablations, rank-k changes, rescaling specific
  neurons/heads/directions) are allowed.
- Do not edit the final norm or the embedding / output layer, and do not only multiply a whole tensor by a constant.
- Never use MATH-500 or GSM8K TEST problems for anything. Training splits only.
- If your sandbox lacks a GPU or time, scale down honestly (fewer prompts, shorter caps) and state exactly what you ran.
  Keep every comparison paired (same prompts and seeds for original vs edited) and report n and standard errors.

## How I will judge it (you never see this data)

Held-out MATH-500 and GSM8K test subsets. Qwen models: chat template with thinking enabled, temperature 0.6, top_p 0.95,
top_k 20, presence_penalty 1.5, up to 4,096 new tokens. Gemma: no thinking, temperature 1.0, top_k 64, top_p 0.95,
8,192 tokens. Grading: last \boxed{} with math-verify (lenient fallback to "the answer is" / last number for Gemma and
SmolLM2). Unedited vs edited on the same problems and seeds, plus the share of answers cut at the limit.
