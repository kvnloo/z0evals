Fix and extend the money package. (1) Adding Money values with different currencies must raise CurrencyMismatch, defined in a new module money/errors.py as a subclass of ValueError. (2) Money.parse(text, currency='USD') must parse the decimal string exactly (no float) and round to cents half away from zero: '1.005' -> 101 cents, '-1.005' -> -101, '2' -> 200. (3) Add Money.allocate(ratios): split the amount into len(ratios) Money values in proportion to the non-negative integer ratios, flooring each share, then give the leftover cents one at a time to the first shares, so the parts always sum to the original. (4) money.invoice.total(lines, tax_rate) takes lines of (unit_price: Money, qty: int) and a tax rate given as a string or Decimal; it must compute tax per line (line cents * rate, rounded half away from zero to a cent) and sum lines plus taxes. All lines must share one currency (otherwise CurrencyMismatch); the result is in that currency; an empty invoice returns Money(0, 'USD'). Make `python3 -m unittest` pass. Do not modify the tests.
