Add stock reservations to the inv package. Inventory.reserve(sku, qty) holds qty units, Inventory.release(sku, qty) returns held units, and Inventory.available(sku) is on_hand minus reserved. Reserving more than is available, releasing more than is reserved, and remove() calls that would leave on_hand below the reserved quantity must raise a new exception InsufficientStock, defined in inv/errors.py as a subclass of InventoryError. Unknown SKUs raise UnknownSku in every method. inv.report.summary must output the header `sku,on_hand,reserved,available` and one row per SKU in sorted order. Make `python3 -m unittest` pass. Do not modify the tests.
