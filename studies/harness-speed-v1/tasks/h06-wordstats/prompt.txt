Extend the ts word-statistics package. ts.cli.main(argv) returns the report text. Add options: `--top N` (default 10), `--ignore-case` (count case-insensitively and report lowercase words), `--stopwords FILE` (one word per line; blank lines and lines starting with '#' are ignored; matched case-insensitively when --ignore-case is given, exactly otherwise), and `--min-length K` (default 1; drop shorter words). Words are runs of letters and apostrophes with leading and trailing apostrophes stripped and empty results dropped. Ties in count are ordered alphabetically. Each output line is `word<TAB>count`. Keep word splitting in ts/words.py and counting in ts/stats.py. Make `python3 -m unittest` pass. Do not modify the tests.
